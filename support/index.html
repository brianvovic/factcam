Address & GPS

Does it work without mobile data?Yes for GPS, time and the stamp: GPS uses satellites, not mobile data. The street address (looked up by Apple or Google), the network time check, ads and purchases need internet. Offline, the coordinates are stamped and the address fills in once you are connected. Underground or indoors without sky view GPS can be weak – step outside for a few seconds.
The house number is wrong by oneTap −1 or +1 above the stamp. The fix is kept for this stop and cleared automatically once you move about 40 m away.
The whole address is wrong or missingTap the address or the pencil to type it. Coordinates are always stamped, even when there is no address (no signal, rural roads). The proof records that the address was edited by hand.
“Finding GPS…” takes longStep outside or near a window for a few seconds. Turn on Location (and on Android, Google Location Accuracy if offered). Indoors and in garages GPS can be off by 20–50 m; the stamp shows the accuracy (±).
The stamp says “mock location”A fake-GPS app is active on your phone. Turn it off in Developer options; platforms reject such proofs.

Time

What does “Time verified” mean?FactCam compared your phone clock with internet time servers or satellite time. If your phone clock is wrong, the stamp still shows the correct time.

Photos, videos & backup

Where are my proofs stored?Inside the app (History). Turn on Save to Photos in Settings to also keep a copy in your gallery.
Photos don’t appear in my galleryAllow FactCam to add photos: Android Settings → Apps → FactCam → Permissions, or iPhone Settings → FactCam → Photos → “Add Photos Only”.
How do I record a video?Hold the shutter button, or switch to VIDEO. Free: up to 15 seconds; Pro: 30 seconds.
Moving to a new phoneSettings → Export all creates a ZIP with every proof, signatures and a CSV list. Save it to your cloud drive or computer.
I deleted a proof by mistakeSettings → Recently deleted. Proofs stay there for 30 days.

Sharing with support

What should I send in a “never delivered” dispute?Open the proof → Copy text and paste it into the support chat, then attach the photo with Share proof. The text includes time, address, GPS with a map link, and the proof ID.
How do I make a PDF of my shift?History → PDF report next to a day. Pro users can create reports any time; free users can unlock a report by watching a short ad.

Trades & tools

I’m not a delivery driverTap the round coloured button left of the status chips and pick one of 12 work types: delivery, construction & repair, maintenance & pest control, cleaning, inspection, rental & vehicle handover, insurance & accidents, field sales & retail, agriculture, security patrol, attendance or personal & travel. You get matching status tags and tools; you can switch any time.
How do before / after photos work?Take the “before” photo. When the work is done, tap the before/after button next to the viewfinder and pick that photo: it appears faded over the camera so you can line up the same angle. The new photo is linked to it, and the proof screen can share both side by side.
Add a job, site or order nameTap the work-type pill and type a job / site name. It is stamped on every photo until you clear it, and you can search History by it.
Show which way the camera facedSettings → Compass direction. Keep the phone away from metal and magnets for an accurate reading.
How can my client check a photo is real?Send the original file plus the proof text, and point them to the verify page. It runs in their browser and uploads nothing.

Subscription & ads

Restore my Pro purchase on a new phoneUse the same Apple ID / Google account, open the Pro screen and tap Restore purchases.
Cancel my subscriptioniPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions. Or in the app: Settings → Manage subscription.
Why are there ads?Ads keep the free version free. They never appear on the camera screen or while you shoot. Pro removes them all.
Change my ad privacy choicesSettings → Ad privacy choices (shown where required by law).

Privacy

Do you upload my photos or track my location?No. Everything stays on your phone, and location is used only while the camera is open. See the Privacy Policy.
Delete all my dataDelete proofs in History, or uninstall the app. We keep no copy.
