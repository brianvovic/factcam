Questions

Does the app upload my photos?No. Photos, locations and settings stay on your phone. See the privacy policy.
Does it work without mobile data?GPS, time and the stamp work without data, because GPS uses satellites. The street address and the network time check need internet; offline, coordinates are stamped and the address appears once you’re connected.
How can a client check a photo is real?Send the original file and the proof text. They open the verify page and drop the file in – it shows whether it matches, plus the time and GPS stored in the photo.
Is the PDF report legally binding?It is strong supporting evidence – every photo has a fingerprint anyone can check, and you can sign the declaration page – but no app can make a document legally binding on its own. Ask the platform, insurer or court what they accept.
Will my delivery platform accept these photos?The app makes your photo clearer and harder to dispute, but each platform decides its own cases. Always follow your platform’s photo rules too.
