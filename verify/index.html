Verify a proof photo

Confirm a photo or video has not been edited since it was taken, and read the time and GPS stored inside. Runs entirely on this device – files are never uploaded.

Paste the proof text (optional)

From the driver’s message, the PDF report or the app’s “Copy text” button. You can paste several.

How it works

When the app saves a proof it records a SHA-256 fingerprint of the file. Changing even one pixel gives a completely different fingerprint. If the fingerprint of the file you have matches the one in the proof text, the file is exactly what the app saved.

Messaging apps (WhatsApp, Zalo, Messenger, e-mail “reduce size”) often re-compress photos, which changes the fingerprint even though nobody edited the picture. Ask for the file shared as a document, or from the app’s PDF/ZIP export, when you need a match.
