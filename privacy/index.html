Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026 · applies to the FactCam app for Android and iOS and this website

In short: your photos, videos, signatures and locations stay on your phone. We have no servers that receive them and no user accounts. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob; FactCam Pro shows none.

1. Who we are

FactCam is made by an independent developer (“we”). Contact: contact@factcam.pro.

2. Data that stays on your device

  • Photos and videos you take, with their stamp, and an optional copy in your phone’s photo library if you turn that on.
  • Location, used only while the camera screen is open to stamp the address and GPS coordinates on the proof and in its metadata. FactCam never uses location in the background.
  • Proof details: status, address, notes, scanned tracking numbers, recipient name and signature.
  • Settings, such as the name or logo on your stamp.
  • An error log of app problems (no photos or locations). It leaves the phone only if you tap “Send diagnostic report” and choose how to send it.

Nothing in this list is sent to us. It leaves your phone only when you share it yourself, for example a photo, a PDF report or a backup ZIP through your phone’s share menu.

4. Advertising and your choices

Ads appear only in the free version, never on the camera screen. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the app asks for your consent before personalized ads, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your phone’s settings. Subscribing to Pro removes all ads.

5. What we don’t do

  • No sign-up and no user accounts.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs beyond the ad and subscription services above.
  • We never sell your photos, videos, signatures or locations.
  • No background location.

6. Keeping and deleting data

Proofs stay on your phone until you delete them. Deleted proofs go to “Recently deleted” for 30 days, and you can set automatic clean-up in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes all app data; copies you saved to your photo library stay there until you delete them. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete or export on request, but we are happy to help: write to us.

7. Children

FactCam is a work tool for adults and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to processing of your personal data. For data held by Google, Apple or RevenueCat, use their tools or contact them. For anything else, contact us.

9. Changes

We will update this page when the app changes, and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
