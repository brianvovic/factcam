2. What the stamp is, and isn’t

The stamp records what your phone reports at the moment of capture: device or verified network/satellite time, GPS position and the address returned by your phone’s map service. Addresses can be wrong, GPS can drift, and you can correct the address by hand (edited addresses are marked as such in the proof’s data). FactCam does not guarantee that a delivery platform, customer, insurer or court will accept a proof. Decisions about your account or pay are made by the platform you work for.

3. Using the app responsibly

  • Don’t use the app to create misleading evidence, for example with a fake-location app or an edited address meant to deceive. Proofs record when a mock location was detected.
